Frequently Asked Questions

Clear Answers to Common Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Bhakti Enterprise.

How do you assist with the 20-tester Google Play requirement?

For individual developer accounts registered after November 2023, Google mandates 20 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before production access. We coordinate real device testers, monitor opt-in telemetry, and ensure your production access request is granted without delay.

Who owns the source code and IP?

You own 100% of the intellectual property and code upon milestone completion. We provide full repository access (GitHub, GitLab, or your internal VCS), build keys, and documentation.

Can you fix apps rejected by Google Play?

Yes. We specialize in resolving Google Play policy rejections, including Sensitive Permissions violations, Data Safety discrepancies, deceptive behavior flags, and Target API level non-compliance.

What is your typical project delivery timeline?

Standard Android MVPs and custom websites typically take 3 to 6 weeks depending on feature complexity. We break projects into agile 1-week sprints with transparent demos so you stay informed at every step.
